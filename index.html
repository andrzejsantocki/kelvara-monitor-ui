Why Kelvara

Your balance can look normal while the system behind it changes.

A professional allocator does not need another portfolio tracker. You need to know when the conditions you accepted at deposit are no longer the conditions protecting your capital.

01 / CONTROL
⌘

Control can move.

Multisig members, thresholds, admin authorities and governance can change while your funds remain deposited.

02 / CODE
</>

Programs can change.

Upgrades and configuration changes can alter the rules your position depends on without changing the token balance in your wallet.

03 / EXIT
↗

Your way out can deteriorate.

Liquidity, withdrawal access, oracles and assets can move against you before social media has an explanation.

Built for self-custodial capital

Your funds stay yours. Kelvara surrounds the position with evidence and a prepared response.

The product is designed around three properties that matter to a professional onchain allocator.

01 / SELF-CUSTODIAL
◈

Kelvara never needs custody.

Your wallet remains the authority. Prepared evacuation transactions return assets to the owner-controlled destination rather than giving Kelvara discretionary control over the funds.

02 / POSITION-SPECIFIC
◎

Every signal is tied to capital.

Kelvara does not stop at “something changed on Solana.” It shows which exact vault and position are affected, what changed, and which response belongs to that position.

03 / EVIDENCE-BACKED
◇

Important decisions have proof.

Incident decisions are backed by current onchain evidence and the position's expected state, so the user can verify why Kelvara raised the alert or allowed a prepared response to execute.

Kelvara ExitRail

Sign once. Keep your exact exit ready.

ExitRail prepares multiple wallet-signed evacuation transactions in one protection flow—without giving Kelvara custody or discretion over where funds go.

One signing flow. Multiple exact exits.

Each transaction is fixed when signed. Change the destination, accounts, instructions or amounts—even by one byte—and the signature becomes invalid.

✓Long-lived authorization. Durable-nonce transactions stay usable until the nonce is consumed or advanced.
✓Owner-controlled destination. Assets return to the wallet path you approved.
✓Verifiable execution. Only the exact signed transaction can be broadcast, with an onchain trail of what happened.
