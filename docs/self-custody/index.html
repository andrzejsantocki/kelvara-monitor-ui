Trust

Self-custody

Your wallet remains the authority across inspection, enrollment, and response.

What Kelvara cannot do

  • Kelvara never receives a seed phrase or private key.
  • Wallet authentication grants no arbitrary transaction rights.
  • Read-only inspection remains separate from transaction approval.
  • Protection uses bounded, owner-signed transaction bytes.
